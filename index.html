How We're Different

Traditional ERP implementations are expensive, slow, and leave you dependent on consultants. We work from a different model.

Client-Led Implementation

We build it with you, not for you. Traditional ERP locks you out while consultants build behind closed doors. We train you on the whole system, week by week, so you can configure, migrate data, and adapt processes without calling us every time something needs tweaking.

Fixed Scope, Fixed Price

The price we quote is the price you pay. No hidden fees, no time-and-materials billing, no scope creep. If we miss the delivery window for reasons in our control, we complete the remaining sessions at no cost.

Industry-Specific Expertise

We work with industrial businesses running complex operations. Not theory, not a generic demo environment. We configure BC against real jobs, real inventory structures, and real production workflows. We speak the language of the shop floor, not the slide deck.
