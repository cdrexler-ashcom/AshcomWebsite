About our process

Skip the ERP Horror Story.

Traditional ERP implementations drag on, blow budgets, and leave your team dependent on consultants. We deliver Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central in full, on time, and on budget - with a fixed scope, guaranteed delivery window, and a model that builds capability inside your business.

How we differ

We deliver Business Central using a fixed-scope, fixed-price, fixed-timeline implementation model - backed by a written delivery guarantee. Our model is simple and deliberate.

On Time

Our program will be delivered within the agreed window confirmed at sign- off, measured as active delivery weeks. If we fail to deliver within the agreed program duration for reasons within our control, we will complete the outstanding sessions at no additional cost to you.

In Full

All functionality documented within your signed project agreement will be delivered. Scope changes requested after sign- off may be subject to a formal change request process.

On Budget

The price agreed at contract will be the price you pay for the agreed implementation.We do not apply hidden charges or retrospective costs. Any change requests outside of agreed scope will be quoted and approved by you before work commences
